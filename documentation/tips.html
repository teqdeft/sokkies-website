Tips, rules & FAQ

The habits that keep the site looking like the design.

The rules of thumb

  • Empty = original. A field you leave empty shows the design's default content.
  • [brackets] = highlight, <br> = line break in title fields.
  • Counts are part of the design. Some lists are fixed: the sock-card row takes max 4 types, the Toepassingen collage exactly 6 cards, the druktechniek comparison exactly 2. The forms won't let you exceed them.
  • One calculator per page. The price calculator is technically unique — never add it twice on one page.
  • Update, then check. Save with Update and use View Page — get in the habit of checking your change on the real page, also on your phone.

Photos & files

  • Upload photos in the Media library and reuse them — no need to upload the same photo twice.
  • Use reasonably sized photos (roughly 1500–2000px wide is plenty). Phone photos straight from the camera are fine; WordPress makes the smaller versions itself.
  • SVG icons (the design's icon format) can only be uploaded by administrator accounts. If your upload is refused, ask an admin to add the file.

Good to know (current state)

  • Forms are demos for now. The quote wizard, sample form, contact form and newsletter blocks are visual previews — real sending arrives in the forms phase (Gravity Forms).
  • The configurator button ("Open de configurator") is intentionally inactive — the configurator app is a later phase.
  • Some texts are placeholder drafts from the design phase (marked in several FAQ answers and descriptions) — replace them with real copy as it becomes available.
  • The language switcher (globe, top right) is decorative until the translation phase (TranslatePress).

Frequently asked (by editors)

I added a section but the page looks odd. Check the section's background choice (some sections have yellow/beige/pink variants meant for specific pages) and whether you exceeded a fixed count. When in doubt: compare with the original design page.

I want a page's mobile bottom bar changed. That's the Vaste knoppenbalk op mobiel setting on the page itself, below the sections.

Where do I change the phone number / prices / review score? Always in Website-instellingen — never in individual sections.

Something is broken and I can't fix it. Don't worry — nothing you do in content is unfixable. Note what you did, on which page, and report it.
