Welcome to the Sokkies CMS

Everything you need to manage the website — no technical knowledge required.

How this website works — in one minute

Every page of the Sokkies website is built from ready-made sections: a page header, a row of sock cards, a price calculator, a FAQ list, and so on. You stack sections like building blocks, fill in their fields, and the design takes care of itself — colors, spacing, and mobile layouts are all built in. You cannot break the design by editing content.

Some content lives in one central place and appears on many pages at once: the sock types, customer cases, reviews, FAQ questions, brand logos, and the site-wide settings (phone number, prices, review score). Change it once — every page that shows it updates automatically.

The left menu — what is what

Menu itemWhat it is
PagesThe 16 pages of the site. Each page is a stack of sections. See Pages.
MediaAll photos, logos and icons. Upload once, use anywhere.
Veelgestelde vragenAll FAQ questions + their categories. Used by every FAQ section and the FAQ page.
SoktypesThe 10 sock types. Each one automatically has its own product page.
Merklogo'sBrand/partner logos for the logo strip and the partner grid.
CasesCustomer cases. Each one automatically has its own detail page.
ReviewsCustomer quotes for the review sliders.
Website-instellingenSite-wide settings: contact details, review score, prices, the mega menu, the promo card, and the shared product-page sections. See Website-instellingen.
Golden rule #1: a field left empty shows the original design content. You never have to fill everything — only what you want to change.
Golden rule #2: the design decides colors and sizes. Where a real choice exists (e.g. a yellow or beige background), the section gives you buttons for exactly those options — nothing more, so it always stays on-brand.
