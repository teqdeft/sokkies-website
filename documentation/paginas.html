Editing pages

Pages are stacks of sections. Here's how to stack them.

Editing a page

1
Go to Pages → click the page you want to edit.
2
You see the page title and the Secties box — the list of sections on this page, top to bottom.
3
Click a section to fold it open and edit its fields. Click Update to save, and View Page to check the result.

Adding a section

Click Sectie toevoegen. A full-screen picker opens with all available sections, grouped by category (Paginakoppen, Collectie, Veelgestelde vragen, …), each with a screenshot of what it looks like. Click one and it's added at the bottom of the page.

Reordering, duplicating, removing

  • Reorder: drag a section up or down by its header bar.
  • Remove / duplicate: hover over the section header — icons appear on the right.
  • Changes only go live after you click Update.

Per-page settings (below the sections)

SettingWhat it does
Vaste knoppenbalk op mobielThe button bar that sticks to the bottom of the screen on phones. Choose: none, one button (Gratis proefdesign), two buttons (offerte + collectie), or the contact bar (call / WhatsApp / e-mail).
FooterFull footer (normal pages) or the short mini-footer (used on the quote/sample/thank-you/contact pages).
Promokaart op deze paginaShow or hide the floating promo card (bottom-left) on this page. What the card says is set centrally in Website-instellingen.

Writing titles: the two tricks

Yellow highlight: put the highlighted part of a title between square brackets.
Sokken [bedrukken] voor jouw bedrijf → "bedrukken" becomes yellow (on some sections the accent color is red or dark — the design decides).

Line break: type <br> where the title should break onto a new line.
Example: Klaar om jouw eigen<br>sokken te ontwerpen?

Any other code you type in a text field is neutralized automatically — it will show as plain text, never break the page.
