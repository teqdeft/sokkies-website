Central content

Enter it once, see it everywhere: questions, sock types, cases, reviews and logos.

This content lives in one central place and appears on many pages at once. Change it once — everything updates.

Veelgestelde vragen (FAQ)

What: every question+answer on the site. The question is the title, the answer is the text field below it.

Categories: under Veelgestelde vragen → Categorieën (Bestellen en oplage, Ontwerp en bestanden, …). The big FAQ page builds its groups, chips and search from these automatically — a question without a category stays off the FAQ page (handy for page-specific questions).

Where it shows: every FAQ section. Sections either pick questions by hand (Zelf kiezen) or show a whole category (Alles uit één categorie — new questions in that category appear automatically).

Soktypes auto product page

What: the sock types (Reguliere, Sport, Bamboe, …). Title = name, Kaartfoto (featured image) = the card photo, plus price, badge (Bestseller/Nieuw), short description and an optional custom link.

Every type automatically has its own product page at /collectie/naam-van-het-type/. The Productpagina tab controls it: page title, description, the photo gallery (first photo = big image, the rest become thumbnails), an optional product video, and the specs list. The price table on the page comes from Prijzen & staffels automatically.

Where it shows: the sock-card rows, the full type grid, the "Bekijk ook deze" slider, the mega menu under Sokkencollectie, and the calculator dropdown.

Cases auto detail page

What: customer cases. The Kaart & slider tab holds what the sliders/overview show (photos, badge, Probleem/Aanpak/Resultaat, card subtitle). The Detailpagina tab holds the full story: intro, "Hoe het ging", specs, result photos, quote and video.

Every case automatically gets its own page at /cases/titel-van-de-case/. Sections on that page only appear when their fields are filled — a half-filled case still looks clean.

Tags & filters: give each case a Type sok and Branche — those become the tags on the cards and the filter chips on the Reviews en cases page.

Reviews

What: customer quotes. Title = customer name, plus the quote (no quotation marks needed — added automatically), the role line ("HR Manager"), and a 1–5 star rating.

Where it shows: all review sliders across the site, including the wide carousel on Over ons. The score in the header ("9.5/10 uit 450+ reviews") comes from Website-instellingen, not from the individual reviews.

Merklogo's

What: brand/partner logos. Title = brand name, featured image = the logo file.

Where it shows: the scrolling logo strip (many pages) and the partner grid on the Partners page. Give a logo a Partnercategorie and the partner page's filter chips pick it up automatically.

Order matters: wherever a section picks items by hand, you can drag them into order. Where a section shows "everything", newest comes first.
