Website-instellingen

Site-wide settings: contact details, prices, the mega menu and more.

Everything under Website-instellingen applies to the whole site at once.

Website-instellingen (main page)

TabWhat it controls
ContactgegevensPhone number (display + international for links), e-mail and address. Used by the top bar, footer, contact cards, contact bars and buttons — everywhere.
Cijfers & reviewsThe review score ("9.5/10"), review count ("450+") and the minimum order ("Vanaf 30 paar"). Shown in the top bar, heroes, product pages and review sliders.
PromokaartThe floating promo card (bottom-left): image, title, text, link, and a site-wide on/off switch. Hide it per page via the page's own settings.
Uitklapmenu (Sokkencollectie)The mega menu: which 4 types are the big "Bestsellers" cards, which types appear in the "Meer types" list (uses each type's Korte naam), and the menu's plus-points.

Prijzen & staffels

The single price source for the whole site: per sock type a table of "vanaf-aantal → price per pair". It feeds the price calculators on home/collectie/werkwijze and the price table on every product page.

Add a row or change a price → every calculator and product page follows instantly. The type names here also fill the calculator's dropdown; the order here = the order in that dropdown.

Productpagina — vaste secties

The sections that are identical on all 10 product pages, edited once: Onze beloftes (3 cards + 6 plus-point chips), Druktechniek (the geweven-vs-sublimatie comparison), Ontwerp-promo and Toepassingen (the 6-photo collage — exactly 6, the design has 6 fixed positions).

Careful: settings here change many pages at once. Check a couple of pages after saving.
